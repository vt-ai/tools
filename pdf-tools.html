Why Markdown, not just PDF or DOCX?

        
Markdown strips away formatting overhead — fonts, layout, styling metadata — leaving just structured text. Feeding Markdown into an AI tool or LLM means meaningfully fewer tokens for the same content, which means lower cost and faster responses, and it's parsed far more reliably than content extracted from PDF layout or DOCX XML.
